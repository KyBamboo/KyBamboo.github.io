Privacy Policy

All KyBamboo apps · last updated 25 July 2026

The short version: none of our apps collect your data or send anything off your device. They run entirely on your phone or tablet — no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers.

Apps this policy covers

  • Ham Radio License Prep — US amateur (ham) radio license study app
  • GROL & Commercial Radio Prep — FCC commercial radio license study app
  • CallDrop — screens calls from numbers that aren't in your contacts

It applies to every app published by KyBamboo, unless that app links to its own separate policy.

What we collect

Nothing. No KyBamboo app collects, stores off your device, transmits, or shares any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Anything an app needs to remember is saved only in that app's private storage on your own device — for example, your study progress in Ham Radio License Prep or GROL & Commercial Radio Prep. It never leaves the device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

On-device information some apps use

A few features need to read certain information on your device purely in order to work. This processing happens entirely on your device — nothing is stored elsewhere or sent anywhere:

  • CallDrop reads your contacts and the phone number of an incoming call to decide whether to let it ring through or screen it. It does not save, log, or transmit your contacts or call information.
  • Ham Radio License Prep and GROL & Commercial Radio Prep use notifications only for an optional daily reminder to finish a practice test, and declare no internet access at all.

Permissions

Each app requests only the permissions a specific feature needs, and none that aren't required for it to function. No KyBamboo app sends your data over the internet.

Children

Our apps are suitable for all ages and collect no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

There are no third-party SDKs, ad networks, or trackers in any of our apps. (Ham Radio License Prep uses the public-domain NCVEC amateur radio question pools and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NCVEC, ARRL, or FCC. GROL & Commercial Radio Prep uses the public-domain FCC Commercial Operator License question pools and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FCC.)

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
