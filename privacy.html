Privacy Policy

GROL & Commercial Radio Prep · last updated 24 July 2026

The short version: this app collects nothing, sends nothing, and works entirely offline. It doesn't even request internet access.

What we collect

None. GROL & Commercial Radio Prep does not collect, store off your device, transmit, or share any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your study progress (which questions you've learned and any in-progress practice test) is saved only in the app's private storage on your own device, so you can pick up where you left off. It never leaves the device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only for the optional daily reminder to finish a practice test you started. You can turn it off in Settings or in Android's notification settings. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The app declares no internet permission, and no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, or any other sensitive data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

There are no third-party SDKs, ad networks, or trackers. The exam questions are the public-domain FCC Commercial Operator License question pools. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FCC.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
